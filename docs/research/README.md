## Scope honesty

A full research dissertation is typically 60,000–100,000 words of original research produced over years. Eight independent 30,000-word monographs (240,000 words) cannot be fabricated as high-integrity scholarship in a single automation pass without becoming padded prose.

This programme therefore delivers:

1. **Pass 1 (delivered):** Full research-structured monographs per role — literature-backed design, implementation guidance, trade-offs, failure analysis, evaluation/proof frameworks, and build guidance — with landmark citations. Combined Pass 1 corpus ≈ **103,000 words**.  
2. **Pass 2+ (optional):** Section expansions, deeper proofs, extended related-work surveys, and appendices until each monograph approaches the 30,000-word target **without sacrificing citation integrity**.
